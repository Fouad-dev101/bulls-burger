The Story

From
Nothing.

"Unbelievably soft buns hug perfectly spiced patties dripping with delicious sauces."

Bulls Burger House didn't start with investors or a franchise playbook. It started with a simple conviction: a great burger deserves great bread, great meat, and zero compromise. In the heart of Nador, on Avenue El Farabi, a small kitchen began grinding its own meat, baking its own buns, and stacking burgers that made people stop and take notice.
